# 10" toolless mini rack v2: CM5 blades, real-GPU node, humanoid blade bay (mockup)

This is a concept mockup, not a released design. Everything is generated by `gen_rack.py` (parameters `--units`, `--depth`, `--t`, `--mode`). `./build.sh` rebuilds all of it (loon → vcad → renders, cut list, fit checks) in about 40 s. The v1 files are not in this repo (superseded).

**What changed from v1**
- Depth goes from 300 to **450 mm**, so a full-length GPU fits.
- The GPU node is now **3U** and takes a real 3-slot card lying flat on a riser.
- The 1U 2×2 CM5 shelves are replaced by **2U blade chassis** with 8 front-loading blades each.
- The blade is a **standard**, so the same module also fits a sealed **humanoid blade bay**.
- The rails are now **3 mm** with round **M6 tapped holes**, which fixes the 2 mm rail web.

## Frame (kept from v1, with two changes)

Axes are Z-up, X = width, Y = depth (front y = 0), units mm.

The frame standard is unchanged from v1:
- 1U = 44.45 mm; outer width 254 mm.
- Hole pitch 236.525 mm and clear opening 222.25 mm.
- EIA holes at 6.35 / 22.225 / 38.1 mm within each U.
- Toolless tab-and-slot assembly: flat 2 mm 5052 sides and lids, gravity-hook trays, slide-lock lids, rear cable channel with the vertical 48 V bus.
- Shelf backs follow the bay standard: datum pins, a 48 V busbar pair, and fingers into the vertical bus.

**Depth is 450 mm** (cable channel to 482, 486 overall with the feet; H 420 for 9U). Why 450:
- From the front of the tray, the GPU deck needs 16 + 25 (fans) + 9 (plenum) + 340 (card) + 2 (bracket) = 392 mm.
- The rear needs about 44 mm of exhaust and busbar zone, plus 14 mm of tray margin. Total: 450.
- 400 mm would only take about a 290 mm card once front fans are in. 450 takes cards up to **380 mm** (the generator's check).

**Rail fix:** the rails are now **3 mm** sheet with **round M6 tapped holes** (Ø5.0 tap drill) instead of 9.5 mm square cage-nut holes.
- The web outside the hole is now 8.74 − 2.5 − 2.0 = **4.2 mm**. In v1 it was about 2 mm in 2 mm sheet.
- 3 mm gives three M6 threads.
- Trade-off: commercial 10" gear screws straight in with M6 screws, and no cage nuts are needed.
- The side-panel rail-tab slots are widened to 3 mm.

## GPU node (3U)

**Layout:** the card **lies flat, fans down**, on the lower deck.
- Its PCIe fingers point to −X into a riser slot block.
- The **IO bracket is at the rear**, and the 12V-2x6 connector is on the top edge (+X).
- A 2 mm mezzanine on 4 posts carries the upper deck: the 48→12 V DC-DC at the front, and the **mini-ITX at the rear** with its IO out the back.
- A **flex PCIe x16 riser** runs from the board's slot, loops down past the board edge through a mezzanine cut-out (x 6–26), and reaches the riser slot block.

**Airflow** is front to back:
- 3 × 60 mm fans feed the lower deck: into the 15 mm intake gap under the card, up through the card's own fans and fins, and out past the rear bracket.
- 2 × 40 mm fans feed the upper deck: over the DC-DC and across the board and CPU cooler, then out the rear IO.
- `gpu_node_section.png` shows this path.

**Power:** 48 V comes from the vertical bus into a 48→12 V DC-DC (≈1 kW brick), which feeds the 12V-2x6 cable and an ATX12VO / 12 V-only mITX board. There is no PSU inside the node.

**Fit check** (from `gen_rack.py --mode checks`, also in `cutlist.txt`):

| check | numbers | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| length | card y 50–392 (340 + 2 bracket); tray ends at y 436; 44 mm exhaust | fits. Max card **380 mm**. Front-fan plenum is only 9 mm (tight, see open questions) |
| card height vs opening | riser block x 18–30, card 150 tall → x 180, + **35 mm straight run before the 12V-2x6 bend** → x 215; opening edge x 238.1 | **23 mm spare**. Tallest card **170 mm** with the bend (≈195 with a 90° adapter). 4090 FE is 137 |
| slot width (vertical) | tray top +3, intake gap 15, card **61 (3-slot incl. backplate)**, 3 clear, mezzanine 2, posts 6, board 1.6, cooler 37 → 128.6 vs next tray underside 134.4 | **5.8 mm spare**. Max card thickness ≈ **66 mm (~3.3 slot)**. 3.5-slot AIB cards (70+ mm) do **not** fit |
| cooler intake gap | 15 mm under the fans-down card, fed directly by the 60 mm fans | OK for open-air cards. Blower workstation cards (A6000/6000 Ada, 267 mm, end-mounted power) are easier |
| **2 GPUs in 4U** | stacked flat with 12 mm gaps, + mezzanine + mITX deck. Budget 178.8 mm | **2-slot (40 mm): fits (+22 mm).** 2.5-slot (51 mm): 0.2 mm, does not fit. 3-slot: does not fit (−20 mm). Also needs an x8/x8 bifurcation riser, because mini-ITX has one x16 slot. Use 2-slot blowers. Side-by-side is impossible (2 × 137 > 222) |

**Power budget (ESTIMATE):**
- GPU 450 W (4090) or 300 W (6000 Ada), plus board ≈100 W, gives ≈600 W per node.
- 9U total ≈ 600 + 2 × 185 (blade chassis) + 20 ≈ **1 kW**.
- The 1U PSU is **2 × CRPS-format 48/54 V supplies** (73.5 × 185 × 39.5, ≈1.6 kW each) side by side, which gives N+1.
- The vertical bus carries about 21 A at 48 V; the 6 × 12 mm Cu bars have a large margin.

## Blade chassis (2U): 8 blades at 25.4 mm pitch

- **Count: 8 per 2U** at **25.4 mm (1.0") pitch**.
  - 8 × 25.4 = 203.2 mm within the 222.25 mm opening.
  - Each 20 mm blade body leaves a **5.4 mm air channel** to its neighbour.
  - 9 would fit at 22.86 mm (0.9") pitch, but the air gap drops to 2.9 mm, so I didn't do it.
- **Guides:** flat 3 mm **comb plates** (laser-cut, open-ended 3.4 mm slots), one on the tray and one under the ceiling. The 3 mm blade spine rides in them with 3 mm of engagement top and bottom. There are no bends and no card-guide hardware.
- **Backplane** is a vertical PCB at y 150 with 8 blind-mate receptacles and an airflow window beside each one. It carries:
  - 48 V from the module busbar (fingers into the rack's vertical bus);
  - an **8+2-port GbE switch** on a PCB behind it, with 2 uplinks at the rear;
  - a CAN-FD management bus and per-slot geographic address straps.
- **Fans:** 3 × 60 mm on a fan wall at y 280, **pulling** air front to back: through the blade panel vents, along the blades, through the backplane windows and over the switch.
- The panels, filler faceplates, ceiling and cheeks are all flat sheet.

## BLADE STANDARD (v0 draft)

One module, two hosts: the rack's 2U chassis and a robot's sealed bay. Everything below is the interface; what sits on the carrier is free.

### Envelope (blade-local: x across, y front to back, z up)

| item | value |
|---|---|
| length | **132.0 mm** from the panel front face to the connector mating face (+6 mm guide pins) |
| spine (rail) height | **76.0 mm** (rail edge to rail edge) |
| body width | **20.0 mm**: 3.0 mm spine plus a 17 mm component stack on +x. The −x face is the flat spine |
| front panel | **24.6 × 84.0 mm**, 2 mm, centred on the body; z −2…82 relative to the spine |
| slot pitch | **25.4 mm** (rack). The bay uses the same pitch |
| mass target | ≤ 150 g (ESTIMATE: 6061 spine 80 g + carrier, CM5, NVMe, DC-DC, panel) |

### Rails and guide

- The **spine is the rail**: 3.0 ±0.05 mm 6061-T6, with both long edges used as guide and heat path.
- The **edge band** is 5 mm along the top and bottom edges, kept free of components on both faces.
- **Engagement:** ≥ 3.0 mm in a 3.4 mm slot or groove. The rack comb gives 3.0; the bay groove gives 4.0, plus a spring wedge clamp.
- **Keying:** the connector guide pins (Ø2, one each end) register the blade before the contacts touch. Radial float is ±0.5 mm.

### Rear connector and pinout

28-position, 2 × 14, 2.0 mm pitch, right-angle blind-mate hybrid on the carrier's +x face, z 20–56. The part number is TBD; the candidate families are listed under open questions.

| pins | signal | notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | **VIN 48 V** | long pins (mate first). 4 × 1 A min |
| 5–8 | **RTN** | long pins |
| 9–16 | MDI0± … MDI3± | 1000BASE-T straight from the CM5 PHY. 100 Ω pairs. Magnetics or cap coupling are on the **host** side |
| 17–18 | **CAN-FD H / L** | management. 120 Ω termination switched by the host |
| 19–20 | I2C SCL / SDA (3.3 V) | host-local only: blade EEPROM (ID, serial, power class) and temperature sensor |
| 21–23 | GA0–GA2 | slot address strapped by the host, up to 8 slots |
| 24 | EN | host enable (pulled up on the blade; the host can hold it low) |
| 25 | PRSNT# | **short pin** (mates last, breaks first), so hot-swap is sequenced |
| 26–28 | SGND | signal ground / pair shields |

### Power

- **Input 48 V nominal, 36–60 V operating.** This covers 12S/13S Li-ion (the robot bus), the rack's 48 V bus, and the arm controller's 48 V bus.
- The blade has an eFuse / hot-swap controller (inrush ≤ 1 A, reverse-polarity protection), then a **48 → 5 V DC-DC** (25 W, about 33 × 23 × 8 mm) on the carrier.
- **Budget: 20 W max per blade** (0.42 A at 48 V): CM5 ≈ 10–12 W, NVMe 2–3 W, PHY, LEDs and management 1 W, DC-DC loss ≈ 2 W. The rack chassis allows 8 × 20 = 160 W, plus switch and fans ≈ 185 W.

### Thermal (dual path)

- **Conduction path (primary in the robot):** CM5 SoC → 1 mm gap pad (6 W/m·K) → spine → both rail edges → host slot.
- Estimated thermal resistance (ESTIMATE):

  | step | R (K/W) |
  |---|---|
  | pad over the ~15 × 15 mm SoC area | ≈ 0.74 |
  | spine, two 18 mm paths in parallel | ≈ 0.33 |
  | wedge-clamped edges | ≈ 0.15 |
  | **total, SoC to host wall** | **≈ 1.2** |

- About 10 W through that path is a **≈ 12 K rise** from SoC to host wall. With a 55 °C wall the SoC sits around 67 °C, under the CM5's 85 °C throttle.
- **Convection path (rack):** the 5.4 mm channels plus a pull fan wall. The comb slots also take some conduction.
- The blade **must not depend on airflow**. All hot parts sit on the spine side or on the carrier with a pad to the spine.

### Management

- CAN-FD carries: presence and slot ID, power on/off/cycle (via EN), telemetry (VIN, IIN, temperatures), boot select (eMMC/NVMe/rpiboot), and an optional console tunnel.
- I2C is for the blade ID EEPROM only, so a host can identify a blade before powering it.
- The front panel has a status LED and USB-C (service/rpiboot). There is **no front RJ45**: all networking goes through the rear connector, which is what lets the same blade work sealed in a robot.

### How one blade serves both hosts

| | rack (2U chassis) | humanoid bay |
|---|---|---|
| guide | 3 mm comb plates, 3 mm engagement | grooves milled in 8 mm 6061 cold walls, 4 mm engagement, spring wedge clamp |
| cooling | fan wall (pull) plus comb conduction | conduction only, sealed (lid with O-ring), heat goes into the cold walls and then the torso or fins |
| 48 V | rack PSU (CRPS) → vertical bus → module busbar → backplane | robot battery or arm-controller 48 V bus → robot connector → bay backplane |
| network | backplane 8+2-port GbE switch → patch / uplinks | bay backplane 4+1-port switch → robot main computer |
| management | CAN-FD to the rack controller | CAN-FD on the robot body bus |

### Humanoid blade bay

- **4 blades upright, connector down**, loaded from the top.
- **Outer size: 109.6 W × 84 D (+10 mm rear fins) × 158 H mm**, about 1.5 L.
- Sized against the rose mini / Booster K1-class upper torso shell in `rose/mini/cad/k1.loon` (210 × 130 × 238 outside, about 206 × 126 × 234 inside). That leaves 96 / 32 / 76 mm of room for the battery, harness and the arm controller.
- The bay is generic: it bolts to the torso by its base or its cold walls. It is not tied to a specific robot.
- **Estimated mass ≈ 1.5 kg** as solid plate. With pocketing it should come down to ≈ 0.6 kg; this has not been designed. The BOM is in `cutlist.txt`.
- Thermal: 4 × 15 W = 60 W sealed. That needs about 1 K/W from the cold walls to ambient, and a bare 210 × 130 × 238 torso shell in still air gives only ≈ 5–8 K/W (ESTIMATE).
- So the sealed 4-blade bay needs one of:
  - the cold wall bolted to an actively cooled structure (the robot's fan or liquid loop);
  - a fan over the rear fins;
  - a **power cap** of about 8 W per blade.
- A passive 2-blade bay (30 W) is borderline. This is the main open risk.

## 6U does not fit

PSU 1 + GPU 3 + blade chassis 2 + patch 1 = **7U**.
- `rack_6u` drops the patch panel and still works: the blade backplane switch's uplinks go out the rear through the cable channel.
- The **proposed full config is 9U** (`rack_9u`): PSU, GPU, 2 × blade chassis (16 CM5 blades), patch/switch.
- 12U adds a third chassis (24 blades) and one blank.

## Open questions

1. **Blade connector part:** 28-pos 2.0 mm blind-mate hybrid with long and short pins and float.
   - Candidates: Samtec ExaMAX/EGBT edge, ERNI ERmet, TE MULTIGIG RT (VITA 46 style), or a card-edge with gold fingers on the carrier plus a backplane card-edge socket.
   - The cheapest is card-edge with no blade-side connector, but it is weaker under robot vibration.
2. **MDI over the backplane** (passive 1000BASE-T through a connector) versus SGMII to a switch PHY. The CM5's integrated PHY makes MDI the easy route. Signal integrity over 2 connectors plus a backplane is unverified.
3. **Sealed-bay thermal budget** (see above). Choose between a power cap, active cooling, or 2 blades.
4. **CM5 SoC contact:** the CM5 has no IHS, so the gap pad sits on bare components of different heights. Probably a stepped pad or a small copper slug.
5. **GPU node plenum:** only 9 mm between the front fans and the card's leading edge. Most flow goes under the card, which is fine for fans-down open-air coolers. A 2 mm fan shroud would help, and blower cards avoid the issue.
6. **3.5-slot cards** (70+ mm) need a 4U GPU node. That is a parameter change (`G['card_T']` and u = 4).
7. **CRPS 48/54 V supplies** in 10" width: two side by side fit (2 × 73.5 = 147 mm). The specific part, the PMBus, and the AC inlet routing are TBD.
8. Carried over from v1: hook retention under transport, lid lock, and blind-mate busbar finger part numbers.

## Rendering notes

- `vcad-render --labels` labels every top-level part, so labelled renders composite a label layer from a tags-only doc over the full render (`lab()` in `build.sh`).
- The tag doc must frame identically to the full doc, because the renderer frames on the **projected silhouette**, not the 3D bbox. Most docs use one blank-named bbox box. The exploded blade uses the union of the real geometry under one blank name, because its silhouette is far from its bbox. Tag cubes are clamped inside the bbox so they never change the framing.
- A blank-named part still draws an empty leader stub. Each `*_lab_bb` twin (same geometry, no named tags) is rendered to get that stub's mask, which is subtracted.
- `+` and `()` don't render in the label font (they show as `0`), so labels use plain words.
- Dark PNGs come out transparent, so `build.sh` flattens them onto `#0b0907`. Unlabelled views are trimmed with an 80 px border.
